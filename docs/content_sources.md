# Content and asset sources

## Editorial direction

The September 2026 redesign presents Hans as a Scrum Master developing toward AI adoption. It emphasizes demonstrated team coaching, changes in ways of working, business analysis and innovation pilots. It does not claim prior AI adoption programs, Copilot rollouts, ADKAR expertise, formal AI training delivery or quantified results.

Content comes from the local cv-brain: career positioning, Klaverblad, Ahold Innovation, Q-Delft, Agile/Scrum skills, education and certification concepts. Certification dates follow Hans’s LinkedIn certification entries supplied on 8 September 2026. PSM AI Essentials is September 2026; PSM II and PSPO I are July 2026; PSM I is September 2017. Older CSPO, TMAP and ITIL credentials are labeled as previously obtained, without implying current renewal status.

The existing site’s broad career dates are retained. Ahold is explicitly a client period overlapping consultancy. No uncertain education dates or degree-completion claims were added. No unconfirmed telephone number was published.

The role alignment is based on [Klaverblad’s AI Adoptie Consultant vacancy](https://werkenbijklaverblad.nl/o/ai-adoptie-consultant), reviewed on 8 September 2026. The AI certification description follows [Scrum.org’s certification overview](https://www.scrum.org/assessments/professional-scrum-master-ai-essentials-certification).

Certificate links describe the qualification. They are not personal credential verification links, which are not available in the source material. Hans’s LinkedIn profile is linked separately.
